# ch-migel-application

Drafts a Swiss MiGeL application form (Antrag auf Aufnahme in die Mittel-
und Gegenstaendeliste) for submission to the BAG/EAMGK-MiGeL.

## When to use

- "Draft the MiGeL application"
- "Fill in the Mittel- und Gegenstaendeliste form"
- "Write a Swiss device reimbursement dossier"
- "Neuaufnahme Kategorie B"

## What it produces

`applications/ch/migel/<lang>.md` — a complete 7-module application form in
German (default), French, or Italian. Modules: product description,
effectiveness (with PRISMA counts), appropriateness, market situation,
economic efficiency (with CEA/BIA results), MiGeL entry proposal, references.

## Templates

Official BAG form templates extracted from the June 2025 published forms:
`references/templates/de.md` and `references/templates/fr.md`.
