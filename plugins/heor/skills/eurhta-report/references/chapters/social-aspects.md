## Domain guidance (extension beyond the source, aligned to EUnetHTA Core Model — Patients and social aspects, SOC)

Address, where evidence exists:

- Patients' perspective: how the technology affects daily life, independence,
  work/school participation, and family/caregivers.
- Usability and acceptance in the target population (adherence, engagement,
  satisfaction findings from included studies — cited by number).
- Accessibility across social groups: language, digital literacy, age,
  disability, urban/rural access.
- Patient information and support needs.
- Any qualitative evidence (interviews, PROM/PREM data) present in the
  evidence base.

Do not fabricate patient-experience claims; where the review found none, state
that patient-perspective evidence is lacking and note it as a limitation.
