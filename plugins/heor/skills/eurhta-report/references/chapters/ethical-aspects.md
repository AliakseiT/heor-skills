## Domain guidance (extension beyond the source, aligned to EUnetHTA Core Model — Ethical analysis, ETH)

Address, proportionate to the technology's risk and the available material:

- Benefit–harm balance: is the expected benefit distribution fair across
  patient groups? Who might be worse off?
- Autonomy and informed consent: what patients must understand to use the
  technology; capacity considerations for the target population.
- Privacy and data protection where the technology processes personal/health
  data (especially digital-health categories).
- Equity and access: could introduction widen or narrow disparities
  (geography, age, digital literacy, socioeconomic status)?
- Resource-allocation ethics: opportunity cost of reimbursement, fairness of
  the eligible-population definition.
- Any ethical challenges in the evidence itself (trial design, consent in
  included studies) that surfaced during the review.

Keep the analysis anchored to this technology and population — no generic
bioethics essay. Where no material exists, state the question and mark it as
requiring expert ethical review rather than inventing positions.
